## 🔬 Featured Research

### FracAtlas — A Dataset for Fracture Classification, Localization & Segmentation of Musculoskeletal Radiographs
*Iftekharul Abedeen, Md. Ashiqur Rahman, et al.* — **Scientific Data** (Nature Portfolio), 2023

An open dataset of 4,083 X-ray images, expert-annotated for fracture classification, localization, and segmentation, curated to support machine-learning research in computer-aided fracture diagnosis.

📄 Paper: [nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02432-4](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02432-4) · 💻 Code & data: [github.com/XLR8-07/FracAtlas](https://github.com/XLR8-07/FracAtlas)
